    About

    Put the words you want to use in the scramble box. Put the passage whose sentence structure you want to keep in the reference box. You can paste both, or open Load text excerpts and take a piece from Wikipedia, Project Gutenberg, or Wikisource. Then hit Generate.

    The reference keeps its word order, punctuation, and common words like “the,” “of,” “and,” and “is.” Nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs are replaced with words from the scramble. Those replacements are then adjusted to match number and tense. A few drafts are scored and the most grammatical one is shown. Use Preserve line breaks for poetry. Shuffle again for another version.

    The Re-verbasizer is inspired by the cut-up writing of William S. Burroughs, including Nova Express, and by David Bowie's Verbasizer, which he made with Brian Eno and Ty Roberts. Parts of speech are tagged with compromise. This project is not affiliated with verbasizer.com, another web version of Bowie's idea.
