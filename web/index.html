About

The Verbasizer is inspired by the cut-up writing of William S. Burroughs, author of Nova Express, and by David Bowie's original Verbasizer software, developed with Brian Eno and Ty Roberts. This version identifies the parts of speech in a reference text, then replaces them with matching words from the scramble text while keeping the reference text's punctuation and basic structure. It is an independent project; see verbasizer.com for another web-based homage to the original software.
